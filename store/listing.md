# Play Store listing

**App name** (30 max): `Ampere: Battery Health & Watts`

**Short description** (80 max):
`Real battery capacity, live charging watts, time to full, records and ranks.`

**Full description** (4000 max):

Your phone always calls a full battery 100%. Ampere shows what that 100% is really worth.

REAL CAPACITY
See your battery's original capacity next to what it holds today, measured from your own charges. Every figure says how
it was measured and how sure it is.

LIVE CHARGING
Volts, milliamps and watts, once a second. What the charger offers, what your phone supports, and what actually reaches
the cell.

TIME TO FULL THAT LEARNS
Estimates for 80% and 100% at the speed you are charging right now. After a few charges, Ampere learns how your own phone
slows down near the top.

KNOWS YOUR PHONE
Recognises more than 25,000 models, with charging specs for over 300 of them.

RECORDS
Every charge timed to the second. 0 to 100, 20 to 80, 40 to 100, or any stretch you pick: how many times, your average and
your best. Live splits against your fastest run.

RANKS, GOALS AND ACHIEVEMENTS
18 ranks from Copper to Grid Master with your flag beside your name, 46 achievements, daily goals and streaks.

ANALYTICS
Level, power, temperature and voltage charts. Screen-on and screen-off drain, a charge map, grades for every charge with
the reasons, heat exposure and wear trend.

WORLD RANKING
See where your phone stands among more than 300 phones for charging power, capacity, wireless charging and speed.

SHARE
Export a clean PDF report, a rank card, or CSV.

PRIVATE
No ads. No account. No analytics. Your data stays on your phone.

Good to know: Ampere measures at the battery, so watts are lower than the number printed on your charger. Some phones do
not report every value; Ampere shows a dash instead of guessing.
