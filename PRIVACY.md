# Ampere privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Ampere is a battery, charging and temperature monitor for Android.

## What the app reads

Battery level, voltage, current, charge counter, temperature, charging state, thermal status, screen on/off state, and
hardware details such as model name, chip, memory, display and sensors. None of this needs a personal-data permission.

## What the app stores

Readings, charge sessions, records, achievements, your settings, and the nickname and country you may choose to enter.
All of it is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. If you have Android backup switched on, Android may
include it in your own encrypted Google backup.

## What leaves your phone

Nothing is collected by the developer. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

- **Standard build:** the app has no internet permission.
- **World board (only in builds that include it, and only if you join):** your nickname, country, phone model, points,
  best charge times, peak power and cycle count are sent to a leaderboard over an encrypted connection and shown to other
  players. A random token created on your phone identifies your entry. No advertising ID, email, location or contact data
  is sent. Turning the switch off deletes your entry from the server.
- **Sharing:** PDF, CSV and picture exports go only where you send them.

## Deleting your data

Settings > Delete all data erases everything stored in the app. Uninstalling does the same. For the world board, switch
off "Join the world board" to delete your entry.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Alerts you switch on, and the background tracking notification |
| Foreground service (special use) | Recording charges while the app is closed, only if you turn tracking on |
| Run at startup | Resuming tracking after a restart, only if you turned it on |
| Internet | Only in builds with the world board |

## Children

Ampere is a general utility and is not directed at children.
